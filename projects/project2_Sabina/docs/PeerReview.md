## Peer Review - feedback

* The concept of creating light calligraphy through coordinated motor movements is visually compelling and has strong artistic potential.

* Repurposing components from a Creality Ender 3 printer is an effective and sustainable approach that adds conceptual value to the project.

* The motion patterns could be further refined to create greater variation and complexity in the resulting light traces.

* The relationship between motor behavior and visual output could be made more explicit, allowing viewers to better understand how movement generates the observed patterns.

* The proposed energy harvesting system would strengthen the project concept and should be further developed in future iterations.
